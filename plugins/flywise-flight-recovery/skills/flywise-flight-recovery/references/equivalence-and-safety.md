# Equivalence and safety reference

## Equivalence dimensions

Evaluate a replacement against the original trip across these dimensions:

- **Schedule:** departure and arrival proximity, overnight disruption, and total duration.
- **Exchange cost:** confirmed airline quote when available; otherwise state that the cost is unknown or estimated.
- **Cabin and fare conditions:** cabin, flexibility, refundability, and seat characteristics.
- **Baggage:** included checked and cabin baggage, not merely purchasable baggage.
- **Routing:** connection count, connection duration, airport changes, and surface transfers.
- **Rebooking feasibility:** operating carrier, marketing carrier, alliance or interline relationship, ticket issuer, waiver, and booking-class availability.

An overall score is a comparison aid, not an entitlement determination. Explain the strongest advantages and warnings instead of relying on the percentage alone.

## Cost language

Use these labels consistently:

- **Public price:** current price for purchasing the replacement as a new ticket.
- **Estimated exchange cost:** preliminary Flywise calculation that the airline has not confirmed.
- **Confirmed exchange cost:** amount quoted through an authorized airline or ticket-servicing channel.

Duffel shopping results provide public offers. They do not independently confirm the cost or eligibility of exchanging an existing ticket.

## Eligibility uncertainty

Final reaccommodation depends on the ticket issuer, fare rules, cause of disruption, airline waiver, booking-class inventory, codeshare and interline agreements, and the servicing agent's authority. Phrase recommendations as options to request unless a connected authorized servicing system confirms eligibility.

## Sensitive information

Treat booking references, ticket numbers, loyalty account numbers, passenger identity, contact details, and payment information as sensitive. Collect only what the current tool requires, avoid echoing it in summaries, and obtain explicit approval before sending it to a new external destination.
